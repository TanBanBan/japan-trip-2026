    Trip debts

    Record what one traveler paid for another, then see how to settle up.

    Enter only the amount paid for the selected traveler, excluding your own share. For more than one traveler, add each person's share separately. To record a repayment, choose the person sending money as Paid by and the recipient as Paid for.

    Suggested payments net all three travelers' balances, so the recipient can differ from the original payer. Recording a payment here does not send money.

    JPY* is provisional yen, kept separate from ILS. Enter the actual ILS equivalent on a yen entry when settling. That entry then moves into the ILS calculation. Its original yen amount stays visible; no exchange rate is assumed.
